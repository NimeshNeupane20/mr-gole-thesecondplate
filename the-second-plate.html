Our story

A place built with passion and flavor

"This isn't just another restaurant — it's a place built with passion, flavor, and the promise of giving you a reason to always come back for The Second Plate. From delicious momos to flavorful keema noodles and more."

In 2026, Mr Gole teamed up with friends to open The Second Plate in Budhanilkantha. The goal was simple: a cozy spot where friends and family gather over hearty Nepali dishes — and always come back for seconds.

Founded 2026 · Owner Mr Gole (Phuntsok Namgyel Lama) & partners
