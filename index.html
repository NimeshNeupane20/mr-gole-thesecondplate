About

A young dreamer with a camera and an appetite

In my own words: a young and enthusiastic boy with a vision to make a change. I started filming the food I love across Kathmandu and beyond — honest, hungry, and a little bit obsessed. Today that journey lives on YouTube, where around 130K of you follow along, and at The Second Plate, the restaurant I opened in Budhanilkantha.
