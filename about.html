About

Meet Mr Gole

Phuntsok Namgyel Lama — a Kathmandu-based food & travel content creator, and a co-founder of The Second Plate. Everyday hustle, every plate a story.

Who I am

A young dreamer with a camera and an appetite

In my own words: a young and enthusiastic boy with a vision to make a change. I started filming the food I love across Kathmandu and beyond — honest, hungry, and a little bit obsessed. What began as simple food hunts grew into a community of around 130,000 people on YouTube, with more than 18 million views. In 2026, that journey became something you can taste: The Second Plate, my restaurant in Budhanilkantha.

What I do

Three kinds of food adventures

From cheap local gems to all-out eating challenges — plus the occasional honest taste-test.

01

Budget food hunts

Chasing the best momo, sekuwa and khaja across Kathmandu, Dharan, Itahari, Jhapa and beyond — proving great food doesn't need a big budget.

02

Big-money eating challenges

Nepal's first-ever bhat eating competition, 2KG biryani runs, and "spend it all in 24 hours" dares — my most-loved, most-watched format.

03

Honest taste-tests

Cheap vs expensive, original vs copy, brand call-outs — tasting it side by side and telling you the truth. Plus travel editions across Nepal and India.
